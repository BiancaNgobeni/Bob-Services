OUR STORY

Built by Students.
For Students.

BOB Services, short for Bringing Out the Best, was founded in 2025 with a simple goal: to provide University of Limpopo students with a safe, reliable and convenient storage solution.

In our first year, we collected and stored over 500 items, successfully returning every one of them safe and sound. We also offer pickup and delivery, making the storage process more convenient for students.

What started as a small idea has grown into a service students can rely on, and we're excited to keep building on that foundation.
